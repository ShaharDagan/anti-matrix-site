RelationshipExpert makes short, original videos about confidence and communication, the psychology of breakups (attachment styles, being the dumper vs. the dumped, regret), and what actually comes across as attractive. One idea per video, explained in under a minute.

About the AntiMatrix app

AntiMatrix is our internal publishing tool. It helps us draft scripts and visuals with AI assistance, and after a person on our team reviews and approves each video, it uploads that video to our own RelationshipExpert accounts on TikTok and YouTube. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
