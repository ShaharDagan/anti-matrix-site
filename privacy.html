Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Summary

AntiMatrix is an internal tool that posts our own videos to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It does not collect data about viewers or any other users. The only account data it stores is the sign-in (OAuth) tokens for our own accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: with the user.info.basic and video.publish permissions, the app reads our own account's basic profile (ID, display name, avatar) and uploads videos to our own account.
  • YouTube: with the youtube.upload permission, the app uploads videos to our own channel. It does not read viewer data, comments, or analytics.

What data we store

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for our own TikTok and YouTube accounts, stored encrypted in AWS Secrets Manager.
  • The videos we create and basic records of what we posted and when.

We do not store data about other TikTok or YouTube users, and we do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties except the platforms we post to and the cloud services that run the app (Amazon Web Services).

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos on YouTube you are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the account's security settings: for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok, under Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens. You can also email us to request deletion of any data related to you.

Children

Our content is aimed at adults, and the app does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
